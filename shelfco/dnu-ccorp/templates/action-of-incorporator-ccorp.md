The Certificate of Incorporation was filed ahead of time by General Legal's designated incorporator, so this document is executed at the moment a client claims the ShelfCo rather than at incorporation. The Incorporator is always General Legal's designated incorporator (never the client), and is the only General Legal signature in the C-corp ShelfCo packet. It adopts the Bylaws, elects the client's initial director(s), and then resigns. It must be signed before the Organizational Resolutions, because the director it elects signs those. Replace bracketed terms. Note: the company name must be used exactly as it appears in the Certificate of Incorporation, including the “, Inc.” suffix and punctuation.
