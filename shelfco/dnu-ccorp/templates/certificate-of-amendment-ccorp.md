> **Drafting note — delete before use:** The document filed with Delaware to change a shelf corporation’s name, tracking the Division of Corporations’ official “Certificate of Amendment for Stock Corporation” template (rev. 08/2023) in accordance with DGCL Section 242. Used only in the ShelfCo + rename variant. Do not reword the numbered paragraphs: Delaware reviews these by hand and reads the official form. The execution block must be signed by an **authorized officer** under DGCL Section 103, and the signer’s name must be printed or typed under the signature line, so the officers must already have been appointed by the Organizational Resolutions before this is executed. Approval comes from the Name Change Consent (Board Part I plus Stockholder Part II); this certificate only reports that the amendment was duly adopted. Filing fee is $214.00 minimum plus $9.00 for each page after the first, and expedited service costs extra. Keep this to one page. Replace bracketed terms.

**STATE OF DELAWARE**

**CERTIFICATE OF AMENDMENT**
